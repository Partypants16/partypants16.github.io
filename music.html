                            How this is counted
                            • A play counts once a song has played for 30 seconds, the same rule Spotify uses. Hours listened count every second, short plays included.
                            • Spotify records times in UTC. They're shown here in Melbourne time.
                            • A skip is a play Spotify marked as skipped, or one I ended with the next button. Spotify only started marking skips in late 2022, so earlier years look lower.
                            • A listening session ends after 30 minutes with nothing playing.
                            • Only totals and top lists are on this page. The raw history stays private.
